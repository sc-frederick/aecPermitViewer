# Florida permit lookup portals

Checked September 24, 2026. These are the issuing jurisdictions' portals. A county search should not be treated as a search of every city in that county. Portal pages that require a session or sign-in may expose more fields after access; the fields below are those established by the linked public instructions or public search form.

| Jurisdiction | Search entry and platform | Public lookup input and status display | Access and scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pasco County | [PasCoGateway](https://aca-prod.accela.com/pasco/Default.aspx), Accela Citizen Access. The county's [search guide](https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/faffdfda-9a8c-44f2-8a33-f6ed15a651ef) routes newer `RESNEW-2022-000001` style records through **Building > Search Applications** and older `21B000001` style records through **Permits > Search Permit Records**. The direct [Building search form](https://aca-prod.accela.com/PASCO/Cap/CapHome.aspx?module=Building&TabName=Building) has record number, site address, parcel, contractor/license, and contact criteria. | Search result records open by record number. The county says to select **Record Info > Processing Status** for a permit's progress. Its separate [global search](https://aca-prod.accela.com/PASCO/customization/common/searchPage.aspx) accepts permit number, address, license number, or contact. [Sources](https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/faffdfda-9a8c-44f2-8a33-f6ed15a651ef), [status instructions](https://cms1files.revize.com/pasco/Documents/Services/Building%20Construction/Instructions%20to%20Reinstate%20or%20Close%20an%20Expired%20Permit.pdf). | Login is optional for searching; anonymous access is read-only. The [portal notice](https://aca-prod.accela.com/pasco/welcome.aspx) says current residential and commercial building applications are under Building and earlier paper applications under Permits. Use this as Pasco County's records, not a guaranteed inventory of municipal permits. |
| City of Tampa | [Tampa Accela](https://aca.tampagov.net/), the city's permitting system. [City guide](https://www.tampa.gov/sites/default/files/document/2026/accela_guide_search_for_permits_by_record_number_ada.pdf). | Search bar accepts the complete permit/record number, for example `BLD-20-0471790`; the city also publishes an [address search guide](https://www.tampa.gov/construction-services/homeowner-resources). The record shows **Record Status**; **Record Info > Processing Status** exposes active workflow tasks and permit-specific comments. [Status guide](https://www.tampa.gov/sites/default/files/document/2022/accela_guide_find_permit_status_comments.pdf). | Public records can be searched without an account. Exempt/secure files need an authorized contact logged in. This is the City of Tampa's system. [City guide](https://www.tampa.gov/sites/default/files/document/2026/accela_guide_search_for_permits_by_record_number_ada.pdf). |
| City of Palm Bay | [iMS portal](https://ims.palmbayfl.gov/ims), Intuitive Municipal Solutions; the city's [building page](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/government/city-departments-a-to-e/building-permits-inspections) also describes the ePermitHub Digital Plan Room for plans. | The city's [FAQ](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/government/city-departments-a-to-e/building-permits-inspections/building-permits-faqs) directs users to Online Services to search permits and inspection results. The [building page](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/government/city-departments-a-to-e/building-permits-inspections) says applicants can view permit and review status in the Digital Plan Room. Exact anonymous search fields and status labels are **not established** by the public pages. | A direct unauthenticated request to `https://ims.palmbayfl.gov/ims` redirected to the portal's login page during this check. The city's [permit form](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/25074/639095891441270000) says registration is required to apply. Treat public search access as unverified until tested in an interactive browser. Scope is City of Palm Bay permits. |
| Pinellas County | [Pinellas County Access Portal](https://aca-prod.accela.com/pinellas/Default.aspx), Accela. The county's [status instructions](https://pinellas.gov/check-building-permit-status-without-a-contractors-license/) route users to **Building > Search Permits**. | General Search accepts street number and street name; a matching permit list opens the selected record. **Record Info > Processing Status** shows status information and each department's review status. A [county inspection guide](https://pinellas.gov/pinellas-county-florida-building-and-development-review-services-phone-inspection-numbers-and-types/) also describes searching by permit number and opening **Record Info > Inspections** for results. | The county [guide](https://pinellas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Pinellas_County_permitting_guide.pdf) says status search does not require a portal account. The [building services page](https://pinellas.gov/building-services/) says county building permits cover unincorporated Pinellas and Belleair Beach, Belleair Shore, Indian Rocks Beach, Kenneth City, Oldsmar, and Safety Harbor. St. Petersburg has its own portal. |
| City of St. Petersburg | [Click2Gov Building Permit Select Permit](https://stpe-egov.aspgov.com/Click2GovBP/selectpermit.html). | The live search form offers **Application Number**, **Address** (street number, direction, name, suffix; begins with or contains), **Parcel Number** (section/township/range/subdivision/block/lot), and **Name**. A returned [Status Detail record](https://stpe-egov.aspgov.com/Click2GovBP/selectpermit.html?permit.appYear=25&permit.appNumber=05001277&searchType=0&validatePermitView=true) shows **Application #** and **Application Status**. It displays the serial without its input's leading zero. The [portal home](https://stpe-egov.aspgov.com/Click2GovBP/index.html) also offers plan tracking comments, inspection results, and permit history. | The [portal home](https://stpe-egov.aspgov.com/Click2GovBP/index.html) says no account is needed for those lookups. Login uses email MFA; contractor history requires an account. Scope is City of St. Petersburg building permits. |
| Hernando County | [New Tyler Civic Access portal](https://hernandocountyfl-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice) for current records. The county's [building page](https://www.hernandocounty.us/building-development/building/) says it began March 3, 2026. [Legacy lookup](https://pvweb.hernandopa-fl.us/) remains for permits submitted before that date. | The live [public search](https://hernandocountyfl-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice/hernandocountyflprod#/search) accepts permit numbers in a keyword field and lists exact permit-number links. A [permit detail page](https://hernandocountyfl-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice/hernandocountyflprod#/permit/d342dc84-196f-4f0e-b14b-1cee8bd7bcc6) displays **Permit Number** and the permit-level **Status** above workflow and inspection details. The county also describes property and contractor lookup on its [building page](https://www.hernandocounty.us/building-development/building/). | The county says legacy lookup is for review, status, and inspection scheduling only; new applications must use Tyler. Public search and the sample permit detail loaded without login in this browser check. Scope is Hernando County Building Division records. |
| Polk County | [Polk County Citizen Access](https://aca-prod.accela.com/POLKCO/Default.aspx), Accela. Direct [Building search](https://aca-prod.accela.com/POLKCO/Cap/CapHome.aspx?module=Building&TabName=Building). County [user guide](https://www.polkfl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/building-accela-user-guide.pdf). | The public Building search form has permit number, record type, site address, parcel number, contractor/license, and contact criteria. Record detail shows **Record Status**; **Record Info > Processing Status** provides workflow status, and **Inspections** provides scheduled inspections/results. The [guide](https://www.polkfl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/building-accela-user-guide.pdf) gives examples `Inspections`, `Fees Due`, `Closed-CO Issued`, and `Closed-CC Issued`. | The [county historical lookup](https://apps.polk-county.net/cmslookup/externalhansendetail/) points 2010-to-current permits to Accela and provides historical search by permit number, parcel number, or address. The county [guide](https://www.polkfl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/building-accela-user-guide.pdf) notes some records processed by Dundee, Fort Meade, or Polk City, so issuer jurisdiction should be checked on a match. No login or CAPTCHA barrier was established for the public search form. |
| Orange County | [Fast Track Permits, All Types](https://fasttrack.ocfl.net/OnlineServices/PermitsAllTypes.aspx), Orange County's own Fast Track system; [property search](https://fasttrack.ocfl.net/OnlineServices/Properties.aspx). [County confirmation of both URLs](https://engageorange.ocfl.net/permitting-tech-upgrades?tool=qanda). | For building permits, select **Permits, All Types > Building Permits** and search by permit number, address, or parcel ID. Open the permit number for details; linked items in blue reveal more information. Required processes marked **Open** mean the permit is not ready for issuance. The property search accepts address or parcel ID and lists permit history. [County FAQ](https://engageorange.ocfl.net/fast-track-permitting-services/widgets/118242/faqs). | Public search and status browsing are available without a Member Services account; applications and electronic plan submission require one. [County FAQ](https://engageorange.ocfl.net/fast-track-permitting-services/widgets/118243/faqs). Building Safety's permitting scope is unincorporated Orange County. [County statement](https://newsroom.ocfl.net/media-advisories/press-releases/2023/02/orange-county-division-of-building-safety-2022-year-in-review/). |

In a September 24, 2026 interactive browser check, [Palm Bay iMS](https://ims.palmbayfl.gov/ims/Find?bNewSearch=true&cat=Permit) redirected its search to login, and [Orange County's Building search](https://fasttrack.ocfl.net/OnlineServices/permit-building.aspx) displayed a human verification image. Because neither allows an anonymous lookup, the app does not offer those two jurisdictions. A person solving the CAPTCHA during each check was considered and set aside; add them back only if public access changes. These observations may change. A public search for [Hernando's sample permit](https://hernandocountyfl-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice/hernandocountyflprod#/permit/d342dc84-196f-4f0e-b14b-1cee8bd7bcc6) and the [St. Petersburg sample application](https://stpe-egov.aspgov.com/Click2GovBP/selectpermit.html?permit.appYear=25&permit.appNumber=05001277&searchType=0&validatePermitView=true) loaded the permit-level status without login. No portal-specific CAPTCHA requirement was documented in the official instructions; the Orange County challenge was found by direct browser inspection.
