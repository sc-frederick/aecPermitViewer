Permit Monitor adapts Aether by Uiverse Design (https://design.uiverse.io/)
from the user-supplied `bad-dodo-83-64e996bc.zip`, with the same warm palette,
geometry, typography and polish as AEC Plan Search 0.3.3. Copyright © 2026
Uiverse. See LICENSE-aether-uiverse.md. The original ZIP is not redistributed.

Inter and JetBrains Mono are self-hosted latin subsets under SIL OFL 1.1;
licenses are beside the fonts. Lucide's FileCheck mark and inline sprite use
currentColor and 1.75px strokes. See LICENSE-lucide.txt.

Shaders 4.0.4 (Shader Effects Inc., MIT) supplies MultiPointGradient and still
FilmGrain through its public WebGPU engine. Rendering is local and frame locked,
with one frame at startup and on resize, no animation loop, no telemetry, and
no external requests. Unsupported GPUs, reduced motion, reduced transparency,
and forced colors use the CSS background. TypeGPU 0.12.3, typed-binary, tinyest
and tsover-runtime notices are included here; tsover-runtime is Apache 2.0.

Earlier Instrument Serif and Inter Tight font notices remain in assets/fonts/LICENSES.txt.
